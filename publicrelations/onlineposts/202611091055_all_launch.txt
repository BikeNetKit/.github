[philosophy]
#BikeNetKit is free and open-source. It provides cities with tools they can adapt, improve and reuse, rather than locking them into a dependency on proprietary systems. We hope to strengthen local capacity and support better long-term planning. Also enabling citizen science.
[activists.jpg, alt="Climate activists"]


[call for dev support]
If you are proficient with Python, help us test, polish and develop our software at https://github.com/BikeNetKit
We would love for #BikeNetKit to grow into a sustained community effort to improve bike/urban network research/analysis/planning.
