[head]
We just launched https://bikenetkit.org/ - The digital toolkit for bicycle networks! 🚲🕸️

Where should a bike network grow? Which gaps should we close first? #BikeNetKit lets you explore such questions in a data-informed way. This platform and software is a culmination of the last 8 years of our research. 🧵

[philosophy]
#BikeNetKit is free and open-source. It provides cities with tools they can adapt, improve and reuse, rather than locking them into a dependency on proprietary systems. By making our methods transparent and reproducible, we hope to strengthen local capacity and support better long-term planning. Also enabling citizen science.
